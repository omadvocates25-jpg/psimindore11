Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated: August 2026 | पाटीदार समाज इंदौर महानगर (PSIM App)

संक्षेप में / In short: Hum sirf wahi jaankari lete hain jo aap khud, apni marzi se, community directory mein register karne ke liye dete hain — naam, mobile number, gaanv/shahar, aur (agar aap dena chahein) business details. Ye jaankari sirf community ke members ko dikhti hai, aur aap kisi bhi waqt apna data delete kar sakte hain.

1. Hum kya jaankari collect karte hain / What We Collect

  • Naam, Mobile Number (OTP se verify hota hai)
  • Gaanv/Shahar, Zila, Rajya
  • Gender, Age, Marital Status (optional)
  • Blood Group (agar aap donor list mein aana chahein)
  • Business/Vyapaar details (agar aap dena chahein — naam, type, photo, contact)
  • Profile Photo (optional, aap khud upload karte hain)

Hum kabhi bhi aapse password, bank details, ya koi financial jaankari nahi maangte.

2. Iska istemaal kaise hota hai / How We Use It

  • Community directory banane ke liye — taaki samaj ke log ek dusre se jud sakein
  • Business listing dikhane ke liye (agar aapne apna vyapaar register kiya ho)
  • Blood donor list (agar aapne blood group diya ho)
  • App ke andar OTP se login/verify karne ke liye

3. Kisko dikhta hai / Who Can See It

Aapki profile (naam, gaanv, business — agar diya ho) sirf App ke registered/approved members ko dikhti hai, sabko public internet par nahi. Agar aap "Secret" privacy option chunte hain (jo mahilaon ke liye available hai), to aapki profile sirf Admin/PSIM Team ko dikhti hai, general members ko nahi.

4. Data kahan store hota hai / Where Data Is Stored

Aapka data Google Firebase (Firestore) par secure taur se store hota hai — ye industry-standard cloud database hai jo Google khud manage karta hai.

5. Aapke Adhikar / Your Rights

  • Aap kabhi bhi apni profile edit kar sakte hain
  • Aap App ke Community page se "🗑️ Delete My Data" button dabakar apna poora data hamesha ke liye delete kar sakte hain
  • Aap register kiye bina bhi App ke zyaadatar hisse (Home, News, Pratibha, Events, Gallery) dekh sakte hain

6. Teesri party ko share nahi karte / No Third-Party Sharing

Hum aapka data kisi bhi teesri party (advertiser, marketing company) ko bechte ya share nahi karte. Sirf community ke andar aapas mein connect karne ke liye istemaal hota hai.
